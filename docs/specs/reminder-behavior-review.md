# Reminder behavior — business review

September 10, 2026. Plain-language companion to the [revision specification](sales-carrier-workflow-revision.md) and [confirmed decisions](sales-carrier-business-decisions.md). **This describes the intended revision for review before code changes. It is not a claim that these behaviors are deployed.**

## What a reminder is

A work reminder puts a real obligation in the responsible person's CRM list and, where linked, reopens the relevant Front conversation. It explains why the item is back, the next step, who owns it and the deadline. The daily email summarizes those same obligations; it does not create another task to complete.

- Scheduled reminders and report emails run at **9 a.m. Eastern on business days**, excluding agency holidays. Ordinary new messages/calls appear when received. They do not wait for the next morning.
- One business day for response work means eight staffed hours, using 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern. The morning reminder comes before the deadline; there is no separate scheduled 5 p.m. reminder.
- Two-day and weekly follow-up intervals mean business days. Weekly means five business days. The 90-day preparation/return and 14-day quote targets use calendar days; weekend/holiday thresholds surface on the preceding business morning.
- Actual work before the morning batch suppresses an obsolete reminder. Repeated messages about an unanswered request do not postpone its original deadline.
- Reply in Front, text through the shared business line or call through Dialpad. Provider evidence records the effort; staff do not fill out a routine completion form, write what happened or choose the next action date.
- Opening a message, reading a report, drafting a reply, leaving an internal note, archiving or snoozing does not complete the external work.
- One actual communication can satisfy overlapping prompts about the same request. It cannot satisfy unrelated contacts, different coverage lines or separate business requirements.
- Once immediate work is handled and future follow-up is safely scheduled, the conversation can clean itself up. No cleanup toggle or personal snooze extends the commitment.

## Salesperson — leads only

| Reminder | When it appears | What satisfies it and what happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Make first contact | A manually entered/imported lead has no recorded contact; first outreach is due within one business day. An already captured incoming request/call supplies its own commitment rather than a duplicate first-contact task. | Send a substantive email/text, complete a connected call or make a genuine completed outbound attempt. The system records it and schedules the next appropriate touch. |
| Follow up on a website introduction | The initial AI email was actually sent and the prospect has not replied after two business days. | Human outreach or a genuine call attempt satisfies the outreach step. The AI message itself is not a human touch. If the introduction failed or never sent, first-response/delivery work appears instead of pretending the lead was contacted. |
| Respond to a prospect / return a call | A substantive message needing a response, missed call or voicemail arrives. Respond/attempt the callback within one business day. | A relevant actual reply/connected call handles the matching response. A genuine unanswered return attempt satisfies the effort obligation, retains the unresolved request and schedules a next-business-morning retry; ordinary prospect cadence resumes afterward. |
| Follow up when the prospect is silent | First follow-up after two business days; second two business days after that actual outreach; later follow-ups every five business days after actual outreach. | Actual outreach satisfies this occurrence. It advances the cadence; an ignored reminder does not. A substantive prospect response switches the next step to handling that response. |
| Obtain prospect information | Required client information, documents or a decision is holding up the lead. A champion's linked request routes the client contact to sales. | Sending the request/chase fulfills outreach. Receiving and verifying the requested facts/documents satisfies the underlying requirement. Existing requests use the no-reply cadence; no separate note is needed. |
| Present the quote | A usable new-business quote becomes ready. Presentation is due within one business day. | The quote is actually presented/sent through the normal quote/proposal workflow. An unanswered phone attempt counts as effort but does not falsely mark the quote presented. The next step tracks the prospect's decision. |
| Keep the prospect informed during marketing | Carrier work is progressing, no prospect response is currently needed, and five business days have passed without a substantive human update. | A normal substantive update or conversation provides the contact. Genuine call attempts receive outreach credit, with the next attempt tracked; they are not represented as a delivered update. Do not demand an additional status email when normal communication already supplied the update. |
| Reconnect next year | The team deliberately uses Next year, advancing the lead incumbent expiration one calendar year. Sales outreach resumes 90 calendar days before the new date. | Actual outreach satisfies the return reminder and resumes the usual contact workflow. Ordinary chasing is paused before that return; ownership, history and independently outstanding requests remain. |
| Fix a lead communication/coverage gap | A send fails, necessary contact information is missing or lead ownership/next work is missing. | Correct the actual problem and arrange valid outreach or coverage. A repaired address alone does not count as having contacted the person; a valid outgoing communication or verified attempt supplies that evidence. Technical repair stays with the assigned administrator. |

While a prospect is simply waiting for carriers, the weekly-update rule applies. Do not automatically create a two-day “no prospect reply” reminder after an informational update that did not ask the prospect for anything. When a document or decision is actually needed from the prospect, use the prospect follow-up cadence for that obligation. Derive this from the existing request and workflow context, not another per-email checkbox for the salesperson.

Example with no holidays: initial outreach Monday → follow up Wednesday → actual Wednesday outreach → follow up Friday → actual Friday outreach → next follow-up the following Friday. If Wednesday's reminder is ignored, it remains overdue; sending the reminder does not move it to Friday.

After binding, ordinary sales lead reminders stop. The champion becomes the client's main contact. Any genuinely remaining new-business coverage still has its own lead obligation; normal renewals/service do not go back to sales.

## Deal champion — carriers and clients after binding

| Reminder | When it appears | What satisfies it and what remains tracked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare/market new business | An existing quote request has enough information to approach carriers, or the applicable carrier submission window opens. The carrier-specific submission deadline is displayed. | Prepare and actually submit the appropriate risk using the normal carrier/quote workflow. A saved draft alone does not mean submitted. Each applicable carrier/risk/term obligation remains distinct. |
| Start the client's renewal | 90 calendar days before actual policy expiration, earlier if carrier requirements demand it. | Begin real renewal work: for example, send the needed renewal-information request or submit the renewal where sufficient facts already exist. That starts the renewal process; missing information, carrier submissions, quotes and binding remain separate obligations. Automatically generating a renewal record does not prove the champion acted. |
| Follow up with a carrier | Two business days pass after an actual request without the required carrier response, subject to earlier fixed carrier deadlines. | Send the relevant chase or make a genuine call attempt. That completes outreach and schedules the next step. It does not claim a missing carrier answer or quote arrived. |
| Respond to underwriting | A carrier has an actionable question/request. Response is due within one business day. | Give the carrier the actual answer, or begin the necessary client-information request with clear linked ownership. Contact effort does not close the underlying underwriting requirement before the requested information is supplied. |
| Obtain renewal information | A specifically requested fact/document/decision is still needed from a bound client. Chase after two business days without a response. | Actual outreach fulfills the chase. Receipt and verification fulfill the information requirement. The champion contacts the client directly and does not hand routine renewal chasing to sales. |
| Obtain usable quotes before expiration | The current risk/term still lacks required usable quotes. Show it as upcoming during preparation/marketing; quotes are targeted 14 calendar days before expiration. | Appropriate usable quote records for the actual term and coverage lines satisfy quote availability. A draft, submission, old quote, declined option or quote for only a different line does not. Calling carriers counts as effort but does not erase the quote target. |
| Present a renewal quote | A usable renewal quote is ready; presentation is due within one business day. | Actually send/present the quote through the normal proposal flow. Continue tracking the client's decision and any outstanding bind requirements. |
| Finish binding or carrier delivery | Authorized client acceptance/bind work or a missing carrier confirmation/document creates a real requirement. Its existing requirement/coverage deadline drives the reminder. | Complete the existing authorized bind/policy/document work and obtain the required confirmation. Sending a bind request does not itself establish bound coverage. |
| Handle a bound client's service request | The client asks about certificates, changes, billing, claims routing or other service. Response/coordination begins within one business day. | Respond and coordinate the actual specialist work. Initial response and final service delivery are distinct: acknowledging receipt or forwarding to an inbox does not mean the certificate/change/problem is complete. The champion remains the client's main contact. |
| Help sales with a prospect | The salesperson explicitly requests scoped help on a lead. It is visible when requested and enters the applicable morning work list, retaining the original deadline. | Perform the requested contact/work; the actual communication records completion. Future ordinary lead contact returns to sales. The lead's sales manager retains escalation responsibility, even though a champion helped. |

The 90-day start reminder does not require completing a renewal in one day. It starts a tracked process whose later obligations have their own evidence and deadlines. The same principle applies when carrier or specialist information is needed: **starting the work and finishing the business requirement are different events**, both recorded through the real work rather than a second checklist.

## Sales manager

The manager receives a daily view of their direct reports' lead work. **Due today** is visibility; **escalated** means the salesperson missed a required deadline. A salesperson's task stays assigned to that salesperson unless someone deliberately changes the assignment or covers the specific response.

- **Trigger:** the first business morning after a missed required deadline, plus ownership/coverage gaps needing the manager's intervention.
- **Required action:** get the work handled by the salesperson, an eligible covering teammate or the manager. Arrange coverage once when someone is away.
- **What satisfies it:** actual required work is performed, or the relevant business obligation is legitimately resolved. Reading the email, saying “please handle,” reassigning or marking a reminder read does not itself complete the overdue work or reset the escalation clock.
- **If still unhandled:** owner escalation on the following business morning after the manager's one-business-day recovery window. The item remains in subsequent morning reports until recovered.

A timely unanswered outbound attempt is shown as effort with a next retry; it is not escalated merely because the prospect did not answer. A missed next retry, unsent quote or other still-unfulfilled fixed requirement can independently escalate.

## Carrier/marketing manager

The same oversight rule applies to champion-owned carrier, renewal and bound-client service work.

- **Trigger:** the first business morning after a champion misses required work or a quote/submission/service commitment, plus uncovered or blocked work requiring management.
- **Required action:** get the champion or a specialist/covering teammate to complete the required work; resolve market-placement or coverage problems.
- **What satisfies it:** the actual outreach, submission, quote, service result or other business requirement is completed as appropriate. “We called the carrier” does not falsely satisfy “no usable quote,” although the report shows the effort and any external delay honestly.
- **If still unhandled:** the agency owner receives the next escalation after one business day. Reassigning does not extend the deadline.

This manager does not receive routine salesperson escalations. An explicitly requested champion assist on a lead remains sales-accountable; it is not automatically moved into the marketing manager's exception list.

## Agency owner

- **Trigger:** required work remains unhandled after the sales/marketing manager's one-business-day recovery window, or a missing owner/coverage/system failure needs owner-level intervention.
- **Required action:** arrange recovery through the appropriate manager/person and resolve the blocker. This report is intended to identify exceptions that management has not resolved, rather than sending every lead back to the owner.
- **What satisfies it:** the actual required work is handled, the business obligation is legitimately closed, or the coverage/system issue is genuinely repaired. Opening the report does not dismiss the issue.
- **Persistence:** unresolved exceptions remain in the next business morning's report. An owner who also fills a manager/producer role receives one combined edition, without self-escalation or duplicated items.

Example without holidays: required sales/champion work is due Thursday → still unhandled, so manager escalation Friday at 9 a.m. → still unhandled after the manager's recovery day, so owner escalation Monday at 9 a.m. Work completed before either batch suppresses the obsolete escalation. Contact attempts, third-party waiting and failed notification delivery are labelled accurately.

## Exactly which reminder/report emails this workflow sends

Every email below is **internal**, sent to the verified staff recipient through the configured Front reporting route. These are sections/templates of one recipient's morning edition, not multiple emails when that person has several roles.

| Recipient / email | When it sends | Why it sends and the value it provides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salesperson — **Your leads today** | Every business day at 9 a.m., including a short healthy/no-work edition when appropriate. | One ordered list of replies/callbacks, first contacts, due follow-ups, quote presentations, information requests, weekly updates and annual-return leads. Shows last outreach, what is needed and a direct link. Helps the person start work without hunting through snoozes. |
| Sales manager — **Your sales team today** | Every business day at 9 a.m., even when no work is overdue. | Due/overdue counts by direct report, actual escalations, attempts already made and missing coverage. Identifies who needs help without reading every prospect thread. A zero-overdue report is meaningful only when synchronization and coverage checks passed. |
| Deal champion — **Your client and carrier work today** | At 9 a.m. on business days with actionable work or exceptions. No empty reminder on a quiet day. | Pulls together carrier follow-up, submissions, expiring terms, missing quotes, renewal/client requests and specialist coordination. Includes explicitly requested prospect help separately. Prevents carrier deadlines and client needs from being buried in email. |
| Carrier/marketing manager — **Marketing and client-service exceptions** | At 9 a.m. on business days when intervention or coverage is needed. | Shows overdue champion work and business risks, the real deadline, attempts and the person responsible. Makes clear whether the issue is missed effort, external waiting or a missing business result. |
| Owner — **Needs your attention** | At 9 a.m. on business days with owner-level escalations or exceptions. | Surfaces what a manager has not resolved and important coverage/system gaps. Healthy leads do not generate individual owner emails. If the owner also has another role, combine this section with that report. |
| Assigned intake/integration administrator — **Assigned exceptions** | At 9 a.m. when that person has unresolved operational work; combine with any other role edition. | Identifies unlinked calls/texts, missing contact/ownership details, failed delivery or incomplete synchronization requiring that specific person's action. Linking the correct account, repairing delivery/coverage and confirming recovery clears the relevant issue. It does not require the salesperson to inspect raw technical data. |

Each edition includes its as-of time, relevant counts, a short list of priority items, the responsible names and links to the original work/full current list. Larger lists say “and N more,” and the full report can be downloaded. Emails are snapshots; opening their links shows the latest state after work has been handled. No one needs to reply to, acknowledge or complete the report itself.

There is **no separate CRM-generated email for each task, escalation stage or completion**. The next morning's same report carries unresolved work and material changes. Front can add a short internal explanation when it first resurfaces a conversation or its escalation changes; this is context on the same obligation, not another task or a customer-facing email. Normal provider notifications continue according to existing Front/Dialpad preferences.

These staff reminders do not send automatic chase emails/texts to prospects or carriers. The previously specified initial AI introduction remains separate. Existing unrelated licensing, billing and finance notices are outside this reminder catalog; consolidate only overlapping lead/marketing sections of older reports so they are not duplicated at 7 a.m. and 9 a.m.

If synchronization or report delivery fails, do not claim “all caught up” or falsely record delivery. Keep the issue owned and visible, and require independent monitoring of missed processing/report editions. A Front outage can prevent a Front-delivered email; the app/operational monitoring must still expose that failure. Repair and confirmed recovery, not merely pressing Retry, satisfy the technical exception.

## Points this review makes explicit

1. **Work reminders and report emails are the same underlying obligations.** No separate email-completion chores.
2. **Outreach effort is not a business outcome.** An unanswered call counts; it cannot invent a quote, document, presented proposal or carrier confirmation.
3. **Waiting on the prospect and waiting on carriers use different contact rhythms.** Do not stack an unnecessary two-day response chase on a weekly informational update.
4. **Renewal preparation is staged.** Starting the renewal at 90 days clears the initiation step when actual work occurs; later documents, submissions and quotes still have their own deadlines.
5. **Managers must get the work handled.** Acknowledgement or reassignment does not stop owner escalation.
6. **Next year changes the lead cycle.** It pauses ordinary chasing until the automatic 90-day return, rather than requiring a custom action date or erasing unrelated commitments.

The third and fourth points clarify how the existing approved rules should fit together; they must be reflected in implementation and acceptance tests so the interface does not manufacture extra work.
