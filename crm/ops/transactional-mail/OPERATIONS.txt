Deployment and safeguards
-------------------------
Main is the production source branch with automatic builds. The retained
codex-hoa-staging-migration branch uses the existing staging backend namespace;
its frontend-only build specification must not be mistaken for a backend

Verification and monitoring
---------------------------
mailVerification has no URL, schedule or API route and normally has reserved
concurrency zero. A deliberate, reviewed fixture check requires an unexpired
canonical UTC HOA_SES_VERIFICATION_EXPIRES_AT window of at most one hour and a
temporary concurrency allowance. The complete fixture is fixed to the owner,
contains synthetic labeled text/HTML plus PDF/XLSX, and never creates business
records. Use a unique reviewed runId; repeating it reuses the same operation.
Close the window and restore concurrency zero immediately after verification.
Verify a real administrator login separately; a login-request UI message alone
does not prove delivery. Never print tokens, links or mail payloads in logs.

Inspect DeliveryNeedsReview, feedback processing/notification failures and the
private feedback queue. Preserve failure events for investigation. Keep the
confirmed operational-alert route. No automatic recovery worker is scheduled.

Mail-only recovery
------------------
ACCEPTED means provider acceptance; independent delivery/bounce timestamps show
later outcomes. ACCEPTED historical Resend rows remain accepted and must never
be replayed through SES. UNKNOWN/SENDING are held for explicit reconciliation,
not retried after a timeout or provider switch. No suppression clears itself.

For an existing RESERVED/DEFERRED operation, first verify its original provider,
recipients, payload hash, retry deadline and business relevance. Recovery is
normally disabled. The IAM-only mailRecovery requires explicit enablement,
active release/workers and the exact operation key/hash. It loads the original
private envelope and rechecks quota/suppression. A historical provider mismatch
stays held; it is not converted to a new SES operation. Login/invitation,
ACCEPTED, UNKNOWN/SENDING, wrong-environment and changed-payload operations are
not recoverable through this route. Never replay a Stripe/payment/business
webhook merely to resend email.

Rollback after new destination writes requires a destination freeze and a
reviewed reverse reconciliation. Do not point DNS to stale source data.
